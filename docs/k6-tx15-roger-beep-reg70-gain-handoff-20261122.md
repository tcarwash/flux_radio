## Why this is worth testing

The user's question was whether the chip can route internally generated audio to MICIN for things like the Roger beep, thereby leaving the shared mic/programming jack available for serial.

**Source inspection confirms a useful independent RF modulation path, but not literal loopback to MICIN:**

- `firmware/k6-aura/build/aura-fd6818b-port/src/device/radio.rs`: `end_tx()` dispatches Roger beep through `play_roger_tone()` -> `play_tone_sequence(..., key_tx=true)` -> `fd6818.tx_single_tone_on(..., true)`.
- `firmware/k6-aura/build/aura-fd6818b-port/src/drivers/fd6818b.rs`: `tx_single_tone_on` sets `REG_TONE_GAIN (0x70)`, `REG_STATE (0x30)=STATE_TX_TONE (0xC3FA)`, `AFOUT` beep selection, then `REG_TONE_FREQ (0x71)`.
- Compare `STATE_TX_ON=0xC1FE` (normal voice, `REG_30` bit 2 **MICIN enabled**) with `STATE_TX_TONE=0xC3FA` (transmitted tone, bit 2 **MICIN disabled**, bit 1 TX on). Thus the internal tone modulates RF **with the microphone ADC disabled** and does not require a mic signal or jack. `STATE_TONE=0x0302` is local-audio-only; this does not mean a TX-capable tone with PA enabled.
- `REG_47` configures the **audio-out speaker DAC/selection**; it is not evidence of a general-purpose TX PCM injection register. The Roger beep is generated by an RFIC oscillator, *not* MCU PA11 PWM routed back into MICIN. The board's PA11 PWM speaker path has **no proven internal return to MICIN** (`/storage5/dev/flux-pcm-research/docs/k6-pcm-tx-path-research.md`).
- The alternative Roger option, MDC1200, runs the chip's **FSK FIFO**, not an audio sample DAC. Don't conflate it with the simple two-frequency Roger tone.

**Immediate opportunity:** Beyond changing `0x71` to select audio tone *frequency* (current TX-15P), test dynamically programming `0x70` tone *gain*. This may give us a useful internally generated **amplitude-programmable audio tone that FM-modulates RF** with serial still connected. It does **not** imply direct RF amplitude control (APC remains a separate mechanism), signed baseband/DC, or arbitrary RF IQ.

## Exact B-chip register evidence / constraints

In `fd6818b.rs`:
- `REG_TONE_GAIN = 0x70` and `REG_TONE_FREQ = 0x71`.
- Existing AFSK code documents tone 1 enable bit `0x8000`, tone 1 gain in bits `[14:8]` and tone 2 enable bit `0x0080`, tone 2 gain in bits `[6:0]`; `REG_72` becomes tone 2 frequency **only in the correct audio/DTMF mode** (it's also the native-FSK baud register). Use B-chip readback and source-appropriate mode; do not change `0x72` during a native-FSK FIFO trial.
- Existing fixed gain is `60`, set using `TONE_GAIN_ON_BITS = 0x8000 | (60 << 8)`. An illustrative first-tone-only gain word is `0x8000 | (g << 8)` for 7-bit `g`; *verify exact enable semantics on the B-chip* rather than treating that formula as a proven RF modulation amplitude law.
- `REG_30` state, TX audio filter settings `REG_2B`, TX deviation scale `REG_40`, PA/APC, and the receive spectrum can affect the observed output. Do not interpret `REG_70` as PA envelope gain.

## Suggested add-on to the *existing* TX-15P test, in order

**Stage A — finish the current phase experiment first.** Keep the existing guarded `tx8_phase_probe.h`, the agent's `tools/k6_tx15_phase_rf_bench.py` and `tools/k6_tx15_phase_rf_analyze.py` unchanged until the currently running experiment is measured and archived. Its current target distinguishes continuous oscillator phase, reset-on-different-word, and reset-on-every-write. Don't add gain changes to this same waveform or it will confound the phase result.

**Stage B — source/emulator-only gain study.** Create a *separate* `TX-15G` optional fixture/module for `REG_70` updates while keeping `REG_71` at one midband audio frequency (e.g., 1200–1500 Hz). Prefer a modest bounded gain grid `0, 8, 16, 32, 48, 60` initially, subject to established RF gain/deviation safety limits. Include consecutive identical-gain rewrites and explicit zero-gain slots. Do not infer that writing gain 0 disables *both* tone paths unless the B-chip source confirms it. Test compile-time disabled-by-default behavior, exact register-write order, no added PA enable, no stuck tone or TX state, run duration, watchdog and full restoration in the **emulator** before any RF test.

**Stage C — attended physical RF on existing dummy-load / SDR bench, only after A and guards.** Predeclare one gain pattern with known 16-symbol pilot and a separate 32+ symbol holdout. Keep tone frequency, TX power, `REG_2B`, `REG_40`, SDR tuning/gain and source bitstream fixed for the trial. Record both raw SDR IQ and exact programmed `REG_70` words/timestamps. Fit *only pilot* for alignment, carrier offset, audio FM deviation scale and per-gain model; evaluate frozen heldout gain-to-FM-deviation law, step-response settling and zero-gain leakage. Repeating old gain values tests whether writes themselves cause tone phase resets or glitches.

**Stage D — follow-up discriminators if gain control works.** Measure whether a sequence of gain values can synthesize envelopes of a *fixed audio-frequency oscillator* without resets, and whether `REG_70` gains can be varied at the rates used by TX-15P. A gain-programmed sinusoid's envelope is **not** a signed instantaneous-frequency-control sample stream. The second oscillator via `0x72` can be examined separately for mixing/beat effects after the single-tone result is understood; avoid assuming its availability during FSK work mode.

## Verification criteria (report actual numbers, not just “tone seen”)

- On physical RF: measured audio tone/frequency and fundamental amplitude-to-FM-deviation relationship at each `REG_70` value; **absolute or calibrated relative** units explicitly labeled.
- Step rise/fall time, transition spectral splatter, residual tone at zero gain, oscillator phase continuity across **same-value and changed-value** `REG_70` writes.
- Pilot-only alignment/calibration with heldout errors/RMSE and at least two independent repeats; compare the same reference across RF captures. Capture and acknowledge receiver/LO clock and FM discriminator uncertainty.
- No unintended PA envelope dependence or TX power changes attributed to `REG_70` without separate amplitude measurement.
- Safe cleanup, module slot backup/restore, live serial lock, firmware build ID and dummy-load/attenuator/SDR verification; do not collide with the other agent's already active serial or SDR session.
- Record **negative findings too**: e.g., gain writes ignored in TX, no silence at zero, transitions trigger phase reset, clipping/nonlinearity, or poor sample/update bandwidth.
