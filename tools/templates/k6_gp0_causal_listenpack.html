Blind GP0 audio comparison

These are modeled GP0-noise examples passed through Sotto v3.8, not radio captures. For each speaker, compare the six clips and rate intelligibility, effort, and naturalness. Labels are randomized separately for every speaker.

Higher intelligibility and naturalness scores are better. Lower effort scores are better. The page saves ratings in this browser; use Export scores to save a JSON copy for sharing.
